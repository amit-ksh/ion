# ion agent teams

ion teams are reusable hierarchies of agents that deliver a project outcome through delegated assignments and reviewed results.

## Language

**Team**:
A reusable group of agents with one lead and explicit reporting relationships.

**Assignment**:
A scoped piece of work given to one team agent, with an objective and expected output.

**Submitted result**:
An agent's completed work and evidence awaiting its parent's review when review is required.
_Avoid_: Accepted result, approved work

**Accepted result**:
A submitted result that its assigned parent has reviewed and accepted against the expected output.

**Output contract**:
The document and declared evidence requirements that an assignment's result must satisfy.

**Review**:
A parent's recorded evaluation of one child's submitted result, with evidence and either acceptance or correction feedback.

**Rework**:
A separate correction assignment for the same agent, retaining the original objective, output contract, access and rejected result.
_Avoid_: Retry, rerun

**Retry**:
Another execution attempt after a confirmed failure before effects, rather than a correction to a submitted result.

**Superseded result**:
A rejected submitted result retained for history and replaced by a linked correction assignment.
